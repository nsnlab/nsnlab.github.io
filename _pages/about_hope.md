## Hope Henderson

**Master of Neuroscience Student**
King's College London & Aix-Marseille University

Hope Henderson is completing her master's studies as a full-time student in the Neural Systems and Neuromodulation lab under Dr. Violante's supervision.

Her interest in the brain and nervous system has been shaped by diverse academic and clinical experiences. After working as a hospital nursing assistant and earning her BSc in Psychology from the University of Idaho, she moved abroad to pursue a degree in Neuroscience from Aix-Marseille University.

Hope previously conducted pre-clinical research in rodents to investigate the safety of a novel non-invasive brain stimulation method. Now, she is developing and piloting PsychoPy tasks that will be used to investigate the effects of brain stimulation on memory consolidation in humans.
