## Junheng Li

Junheng Li is a postdoctoral researcher in the Neural Systems and Neuromodulation Lab, on a joint appointment with Imperial College London and the UK Dementia Research Institute (UK DRI). Trained as an engineer (BEng and MSc with Distinction in signal processing), Junheng completed a PhD in neuroscience at Imperial College London and the UK DRI, developing a computational framework that provided the first experimental evidence that the brain's wake-to-sleep transition follows a nonlinear bifurcation (Nature Neuroscience, 2025).

In the lab, this work extends to neuromodulation, combining EEG dynamics with temporal interference stimulation (TIS) to uncover how stimulation during sleep shapes memory consolidation. Junheng also leads a UK DRI–LifeArc Translational Pilot Award as principal investigator, developing sleep-onset EEG biomarkers for the early detection of neurodegenerative disease, and has been awarded a King's Prize Fellowship, due to start soon.
