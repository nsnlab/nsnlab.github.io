## Inês Dias

Inês Dias is a Visiting Research Fellow at King's College London, supported by a Postdoc.Mobility fellowship awarded by the Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF). Trained as a biomedical engineer (BSc and MSc), during her PhD in Neuroscience at ETH Zurich she developed and validated a non-invasive mouse closed-loop auditory stimulation (mCLAS) system that delivers phase-targeted auditory stimuli during sleep in real-time, which she applied in Alzheimer's and Parkinson's disease models.

At King's, Dr. Dias leads TILES (Temporal Interference for Localised Event-triggered Sleep Stimulation), a multi-target closed-loop neuromodulation system targeting hippocampal and thalamic activity during sleep by integrating real-time EEG with adaptive temporal interference stimulation. Her project aims to advance personalised non-invasive interventions for neurological conditions marked by sleep disturbances.
